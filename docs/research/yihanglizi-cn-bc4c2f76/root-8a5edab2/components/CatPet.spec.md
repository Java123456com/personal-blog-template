# CatPet

- Reference: `https://yihanglizi.cn/`
- Placement: fixed to the bottom of the viewport on every route.
- Sprite: 8 columns × 10 rows, 32px source cells, rendered at 128px on desktop and 64px on small screens.

## Behaviour

- Wanders automatically using walk, run, idle, and pose rows.
- Click: random action and optional speech bubble.
- Drag: horizontal placement with lifted sprite and landing action.
- Long press (550ms): plays all showcase action rows.
- `Shift+W`: patrol; `Shift+R`: run; `Shift+J`: jump; `Shift+P`: random idle pose.
- `Shift+C`: showcase; `Shift+H`: show the instruction panel for eight seconds.
- `Shift+0…9`: preview the matching sprite row.
- Respects reduced motion and the site's interaction sound and volume settings.
